# State Space

- A state space model is formed by a collection of differential equations that predict the dynamic behavior of a system (or vehicle).

- The state vector includes variables that are needed to determine the system "state".
	- The state vector includes motion variable (position, velocity,...)
	- But may include variables that describe the state of vehicle subsystems
		- Engine ${}\to{}$ temperature, pressure, thrust, rpm
		- Structural ${}\to{}$ deformation, stress
		- Aerodynamics ${}\to{}$ unsteady effects
		- Controls ${}\to{}$ sensors, noise filters, surface deflections, momentum wheel speeds


# State Space Format

- The differential equations are arranged in a form that returns the state vector derivative.

$$
\begin{array}{>{\displaystyle}l>{\displaystyle}l}
\boxed{\dot{\mathbf{\vec{x}}}(t)=f[\mathbf{\vec{x}}(t)]} &  &  &  & \boxed{\dot{\mathbf{x}}=f(\mathbf{x})}_{\rlap{f:\mathbb{R}^{n}\to \mathbb{R}^{n}}}
\end{array}
$$
- An optional measurement or output function may be used to compute other variables of interest.
$$
\begin{array}{>{\displaystyle}l>{\displaystyle}l}
\boxed{\mathbf{\vec{y}}(t)=h[\mathbf{\vec{x}}(t)]} &  &  &  & \boxed{\mathbf{y}=h(\mathbf{x})}_{\rlap{h:\mathbb{R}^{n}\to \mathbb{R}^{m}}}
\end{array}
$$

# Rotating Machinery

- Rotational motion is analyzed using the moment equations of motion.

$$
\begin{array}{>{\displaystyle}l>{\displaystyle}l>{\displaystyle}l>{\displaystyle}l}
\text{Applied Moments }  &  & \mathbf{\vec{G}}^{B}=\dot{\mathbf{\vec{h}}}^{B}+\Omega \mathbf{\vec{h}}^{B} & & \mathbf{\vec{G}}^{B}=\begin{bmatrix}
L \\
M \\
N
\end{bmatrix} 
 &  & \Omega=\begin{bmatrix}
 0 & -r & q  \\
 r & 0 & -p \\
 -q & p & 0
 \end{bmatrix} 
\end{array}
$$
- Any rotating machinery must be added to the total angular momentum of the vehicle.
	- Examples of rotating machinery include:
		- Large radial engines
		- Rotating engine nacelles (XV-15 and V-22)     ${}I^{B}=\begin{bmatrix}I_{xx} & -I_{xy} & -I_{zx} \\ -I_{xy} & I_{yy} & -I_{yz} \\  -I_{zx} & -I_{yz} & I_{zz}  \end{bmatrix}{}$
		- Spacecraft reaction wheels (SAMPEX)



$$
\begin{array}{>{\displaystyle}l>{\hspace{5em}}>{\displaystyle}l>{\hspace{5em}}>{\displaystyle}l}
\overset{ \text{Total Angular Momentum} }{ \mathbf{\vec{h}}^{B}=I^{B}\mathbf{\vec{\omega}}^{B}+\mathbf{\vec{h}_{r}}^{B}  }& \overset{ \text{Rotor Angular Momentum} }{ \mathbf{\vec{h}_{r}}^{B}=\begin{bmatrix}
h_{x} \\
h_{y} \\
h_{z}
\end{bmatrix} } & \mathbf{\vec{\omega}}^{B}=\begin{bmatrix}
p \\
q \\
r
\end{bmatrix}
\end{array}
$$


# Moment Equations (with Rotors)

- As before, the moment equations are found by differentiating the total vehicle angular momentum.

$$
\begin{array}{>{\displaystyle}l>{\hspace{5em}\displaystyle}l}
\mathbf{\vec{h}}^{B}=I^{B}\mathbf{\vec{\omega}}^{B}+\mathbf{\vec{h}_{r}}^{B} & \mathbf{\vec{G}}^{B}=\dot{\mathbf{\vec{h}}}^{B}+\Omega \mathbf{\vec{h}}^{B} 
\end{array}\\ \\
\mathbf{\vec{G}}^{B}=\cancel{ \dot{I}^{B} }\mathbf{\vec{\omega}}^{B}+I^{B}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{\omega}}}^{B}+\cancel{ \dot{\mathbf{\vec{h}}}_{\mathbf{r}}^{B} }+\Omega(I^{B}\mathbf{\vec{\omega}}^{B}+ \mathbf{\vec{h_{r}}}^{B})

$$
$$
{\small
\begin{bmatrix}
L \\
M \\
N
\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}
I_{xx} & -I_{xy} & -I_{zx} \\
-I_{xy} & I_{yy} & -I_{yz} \\
-I_{zx} & -I_{yz} & I_{zz}
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
\dot{p} \\
\dot{q} \\
\dot{r}
\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}
0 & -r & q \\
r & 0 & -p \\
-q & p & 0 \\
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
I_{xx} & -I_{xy} & -I_{zx} \\
-I_{xy} & I_{yy} & -I_{yz} \\
-I_{zx} & -I_{yz} & I_{zz}
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
p \\
q \\
r
\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}
0 & -r & q \\
r & 0 & -p  \\
-q & p & 0
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
h_{x} \\
h_{y} \\
h_{z}
\end{bmatrix}}

$$

# Moment Equations Expanded

$$
\begin{array}{>{\displaystyle}l}
L = I_{xx}\dot{p}-I_{yz}(q^{2}-r^{2})-I_{zx}(\dot{r}+pq)-I_{xy}(\dot{q}-rp)-(I_{yy}-I_{zz})qr+qh_{z}-rh_{y} \\
M=I_{yy}\dot{q}-I_{zx}(r^{2}-p^{2})-I_{xy}(\dot{p}+qr)-I_{yz}(\dot{r}-pq)-(I_{zz}-I_{xx})rp+rh_{x}-ph_{z} \\
N=I_{zz}\dot{r}-I_{xy}(p^{2}-q^{2})-I_{yz}(\dot{q}+rp)-I_{zx}(\dot{p}-qr)-(I_{xx}-I_{yy})pq+ph_{y}-qh_{x}
\end{array}
$$
- Simplifications for the SAMPEX Spacecraft:
$$
\begin{array}{>{\displaystyle}l>{\hspace{2em}\displaystyle}l>{\hspace{2em}\displaystyle}l>{\hspace{2em}\displaystyle}l}
I_{xy}=I_{zx}=0 & h_{x}=h_{z}=0 & h_{y}=I_{r}\omega_{r} & \begin{array}{>{\displaystyle}c}
I_{r}=\text{reaction wheel moment of inertia} \\
\omega_{r}=\text{reaction wheel angular rate}
\end{array} \\
\end{array}
\\
\\
\boxed{\begin{array}{>{\displaystyle}l}
L=I_{xx}\dot{p}-I_{yz}(q^{2}-r^{2})-(I_{yy}-I_{zz})qr-rI_{r}\omega_{r} \\
M = I_{yy}\dot{q}-I_{yz}(\dot{r}-pq)-(I_{zz}-I_{xx})rp \\
N = I_{zz}\dot{r}-I_{yz}(\dot{q}+rp)-(I_{xx}-I_{yy})pq+pI_{r}\omega_{r}
\end{array}}
$$

# SAMPEX Equations of Motion

- Spacecraft engineers have their own notation (they do not like p, q, r, ...)

$$
\begin{array}{>{\displaystyle}l>{\hspace{2em}\displaystyle}l>{\hspace{2em}\displaystyle}l}
\mathbf{\vec{\omega}}^{B}=\begin{bmatrix}
p \\
q \\
r 
\end{bmatrix}\to \begin{bmatrix}
\omega_{1} \\
\omega_{2} \\
\omega_{3}
\end{bmatrix}  & \mathbf{\vec{G}}^{B}=\begin{bmatrix}
L \\
M \\
N 
\end{bmatrix}\to \begin{bmatrix}
N_{1} \\
N_{2} \\
N_{3}
\end{bmatrix} & \boxed{\begin{array}{>{\displaystyle}c}
I_{11}=15.516\,\mathrm{kg\,m} ^{2} \\
I_{22} = 21.621\,\mathrm{kg\,m} ^{2} \\
I_{33} = 15.234\,\mathrm{kg\,m} ^{2} \\
I_{23} = 0.194\,\mathrm{kg\,m} ^{2} \\
I_{r} = 0.0041488\,\mathrm{kg\,m} ^{2}
\end{array}}
\end{array}

$$
$$
\begin{array}{>{\displaystyle}l}
N_{1} = I_{11}\dot{\omega}_{1}-I_{23}(\omega_{2}^{2}-\omega_{3}^{2})-(I_{22}-I_{33})\omega_{2}\omega_{3}-I_{r}\omega_{r}\omega_{3} \\
N_{2}=I_{22}\dot{\omega}_{2}-I_{23}(\dot{\omega}_{3}-\omega_{1}\omega_{2})-(I_{33}-I_{11})\omega_{3}\omega_{1} \\
N_{3}=I_{33}\dot{\omega}_{3}-I_{23}(\dot{\omega}_{2}+\omega_{3}\omega_{1})-(I_{11}-I_{22})\omega_{1}\omega_{2}+I_{r}\omega_{r}\omega_{1}
\end{array}
$$



# SAMPEX State Space Model

$$
\begin{array}{>{\displaystyle}l>{\hspace{1em}\displaystyle}r>{\hspace{1em}\displaystyle}l>{\hspace{1em}\displaystyle}l} 
\mathbf{\vec{x}}=\begin{bmatrix}
\omega_{1} \\
\omega_{2} \\
\omega_{3} \\
\omega_{r}
\end{bmatrix} & \begin{array}{>{\displaystyle}l}
\text{Angular rate about 1-axis [rad/s] }  \\
\text{Angular rate about 2-axis [rad/s] }  \\
\text{Angular rate about 3-axis [rad/s] }  \\
\text{Reaction wheel angular rate [rad/s] } 
\end{array} \\ \\
\mathbf{\vec{u}}=\begin{bmatrix}
N_{1} \\
N_{2} \\
N_{3}
\end{bmatrix} & \begin{array}{>{\displaystyle}l}
\text{Disturbance torque about 1-axis [N] }  \\
\text{Disturbance torque about 2-axis [N] }  \\
\text{Disturbance torque about 3-axis [N] } 
\end{array} \\ \\
 & 
\raise{3em}\boxed{\dot{\mathbf{\vec{x}}}(t)=f[\mathbf{\vec{x}}(t),\mathbf{\vec{u}}(t)]}  & \raise{3em}{\Huge\Leftrightarrow} &\smash{\raise{5em}\begin{array}{>{\displaystyle}l}
\dot{\omega}_{1}=\frac{I_{22}-I_{33}}{I_{11}}\omega_{2}\omega_{3}+\frac{I_{r}}{I_{11}}\omega_{3}\omega_{r}+\frac{N_{1}}{I_{11}} \\
\dot{\omega}_{2}=\frac{I_{33}-I_{11}}{I_{22}}\omega_{1}\omega_{3}+\frac{N_{2}}{I_{22}} \\
\dot{\omega}_{3}=\frac{I_{11}-I_{22}}{I_{33}}\omega_{1}\omega_{2}-\frac{I_{r}}{I_{33}}\omega_{1}\omega_{r}+\frac{N_{3}}{I_{33}} \\
\dot{\omega}_{r}=0
\end{array}}\\
 & \text{note (for simplicty):}\quad I_{23}\approx 0
\end{array}
$$
