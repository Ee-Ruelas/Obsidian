# Position of a Point on the Vehicle

- What is the position of point ${}m{}$ (attached to the vehicle) in ECEF coordinates?

$$
\begin{array}{ll} 
\mathbf{\vec{r}_{m}}^{E}=\mathbf{\vec{r}_{o}}^{E} + \mathbf{\vec{r}}^{E}+\mathbf{\vec{r}_{b}}^{E} \\
\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{E}=T_{L 2E}\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{L} \\
\mathbf{\vec{r}_{b}}^{E}=T_{L 2E}\mathbf{\vec{r}_{b}}^{L} & \mathbf{\vec{r}_{b}}^{L}=T_{B 2L}\mathbf{\vec{r}_{b}}^{B} \\
\end{array}
