# Level Turning Flight

- The forces acting on the airplane are best expressed in a reference frame attached to the vehicle and, therefore, rotating with it.
![[MAE - 142 - 9-1794311201685.webp|244]]Down and ${}z^{R}{}$ are coincident (into page)
$$
\begin{array}{c} 
L = \text{lift} \\
W = \text{weight} \\
\mu = \text{bank angle} \\
V = \text{airspeed} \\
R = \text{turn radius} \\
m = \text{mass}
\end{array}
$$




# Newton's Law in Rotating Frame

- The forces acting on the airplane are represented in the rotating (R) reference frame.

$$
\begin{array}{lllll}
\begin{array}{l}
\text{Forces in\\Rotating \\Frame}
\end{array}  & \mathbf{\vec{F}}^{R}=\begin{bmatrix}
-L\sin \mu \\
T-D & W-L\cos \mu 
\end{bmatrix}
 & \begin{array}{l}
 \text{Velocity in\\Rotating\\Frame}
 \end{array} & \mathbf{\vec{v}}^{R}=\begin{bmatrix}
 0 \\
 V \\
 0
 \end{bmatrix} & 
 \begin{array}{c}
 L = \text{lift} \\
 W = \text{weight} \\
 \mu = \text{bank angle} \\
 V = \text{airspeed} \\
 R = \text{turn radius} \\
 m = \text{mass}
 \end{array}
\end{array}
$$

- Assume the local NED frame is an inertial frame. Newton's Law becomes:

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
m \dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{L} =\mathbf{\vec{F}}^{L}  &  mT_{L 2 R}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{L}=T_{L 2 R}\mathbf{\vec{F}}^{L}= \mathbf{\vec{F}}^{R}  
\end{array}
\\
\boxed{mT_{L 2 R}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{L}= \mathbf{\vec{F}}^{R}}
$$
# Acceleration

- Find the acceleration of the vehicle in the local frame and then rotate the result into the moving frame.

$$
\begin{array}{llll}
 & \mathbf{\vec{v}}^{L}=T_{R 2 L}\mathbf{\vec{v}}^{R} \\
 \text{differentiate} & \dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{L}=T_{R 2 L}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{R}+\dot{T}_{R 2 L}\mathbf{\vec{v}}^{R} \\
 \text{rotate}  & T_{L 2 R}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{L}=T_{L 2 R}T_{R 2 L}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{R}+T_{L 2 R}\dot{T}_{R 2 L}\mathbf{\vec{v}}^{R} \\
  & \boxed{T_{L 2 R}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{L}=\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{R}+T_{L 2 R}\dot{T}_{R 2 L}\mathbf{\vec{v}}^{R}}  & \mathbf{\vec{v}}^{R}=\begin{bmatrix}
  0 \\
  V \\
  0
  \end{bmatrix} & \dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{R}=\begin{bmatrix}
  0 \\
  \dot{V} \\
  0
  \end{bmatrix}
\end{array}
$$
