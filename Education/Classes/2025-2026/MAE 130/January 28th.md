$u_n(t)$: homogeneous solution: the solution to the case of $f(t) = 0$ 
$u_p(t)$: particular solution: the solution to the case of $f(t) \neq 0$

Assume the particular solution follows the general function:

$$
m\ddot{u} + c\dot{u} + ku + Fe^{i\omega t}, \quad u_p(t)=Ae^{i\omega t}
$$

$$
u(t) = e^{-5\omega_0 t}[C_1 \cos{(\omega_0t)} + C_2 \sin{(\omega_0t)}]
