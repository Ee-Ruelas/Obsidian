## Radiation Exchange between Black Surfaces: 

![[MAE - 101 - 6-1793572409543.webp|455]]${}T_{1} > T_{2}{}$












Net Heat Exchange ${}\boxed{\dot{Q}_{1} - \dot{Q}_{2} = \dot{Q}_{12}}{}$

View Factor (Shape Factor):
$$
\begin{align}
 & F_{12} = \frac{\text{Radiation from \(A_{1}\) that is intercepted  by \(A_{2}\)}}{\text{Total radiation leaving \(A_{1}\)}}\\
 & F_{12} = \frac{\dot{Q}_{A_{1}\to A_{2}}}{\dot{Q}_{A_{1}}}\\
 & F_{21} = \frac{\text{Radiation from \(A_{2}\) that is intercepted by \(A_{1}\)}}{\text{Total Radiation leaving \(A_{2}\)}}\\
  & \quad \quad \quad \quad \quad \quad \quad \underset{\circledast}{\downarrow} 
\end{align}
$$
Net exchange:
$$
\begin{gather}
\boxed{\dot{Q}_{12} = (E_{b_{1}}A_{1})F_{12} - (\overset{ \circledast }{ E_{b_{2}}A_{2} })F_{21}} \\
\boxed{E_{b}= \sigma T^{4}}
\end{gather}
$$
Note:
If ${}T_{1}=T_{2} \implies E_{b_{1}} = E_{b_{2}}{}$
${}*{}$Net Exchange ${}\dot{Q}_{12}=0{}$
The result is:
$$
\begin{array}{ll}
\boxed{A_{1}F_{12} = A_{2}F_{21}}  &  \substack{\text{``Reciprocal Rule" \\ General Equation} }
\end{array}

$$
Even if the surface temperature are not the same, the reciprocal rule still applies.
(Valid for any two surfaces)
