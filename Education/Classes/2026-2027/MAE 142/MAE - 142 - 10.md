# Force Equations

- Use Newton's Law to develop the force equations in the body-fixed frame.

$$
\begin{array}{c}
\boxed{m\mathbf{\vec{a}}^{B}=\mathbf{\vec{F}}^{B}+m\mathbf{\vec{g}}^{B}} \\ 
\swarrow\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad \searrow \\
\begin{array}{lr}
\mathbf{\vec{a}}^{B}=T_{L 2 B}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{L}=\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{B}+\Omega \mathbf{\vec{v}}^{B} & &  &  \mathbf{\vec{g}}^{B}=T_{L 2 B}\mathbf{\vec{g}}^{L}
\end{array} \\
m\begin{bmatrix}
\dot{u} \\
\dot{v} \\
\dot{w} 
\end{bmatrix}+m\begin{bmatrix}
0 & -r & q \\
r & 0 & -p \\
-q & p & 0
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
u \\
v \\
w
\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}
F_{x} \\
F_{y} \\
F_{z}
\end{bmatrix}+m\begin{bmatrix}
-g\sin \theta \\
g\sin \phi \cos \theta \\
g\cos \phi \cos \theta
\end{bmatrix} \\
\boxed{\begin{array}{c}
m(\dot{u}+qw-rv)=F_{x}-mg\sin \theta \\
m(\dot{v}+ru-pw)=F_{y}+mg\cos \theta \sin \phi \\
m(\dot{w}+pv-qu)=F_{z}+mg\cos \theta \cos \phi
\end{array}}
\end{array}
$$

# Angular Momentum

- Start with an expression for angular momentum

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
\boxed{\mathbf{\vec{h}}^{B}=I^{B}\mathbf{\vec{\omega}}} & \mathbf{\vec{\omega}}=\begin{bmatrix}
p \\
q \\
r
\end{bmatrix} \\
I^{B}=\begin{bmatrix}
I_{xx} & -I_{xy} & -I_{zx} \\
-I_{xy} & I_{yy} & -I_{yz} \\
-I_{zx} & -I_{yz} & I_{zz}
\end{bmatrix} & \begin{array}{ll}
I_{xx}=\int(y^{2}+z^{2})dm  & \text{etc.} \\
I_{xy}=\int xy\quad dm & \text{etc.}
\end{array}
\end{array}
$$
- The equations of rotational motion are found by setting the applied moments equal to the angular momentum rate.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathbf{\vec{h}}^{L}=T_{B 2 L}\mathbf{\vec{h}}^{B}\to \boxed{T_{L 2 B}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{h}}}^{L}=\dot{\mathbf{\vec{h}}}^{B}+\Omega \mathbf{\vec{h}}^{B}} & \boxed{T_{L 2 B}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{h}}}^{L}=\mathbf{\vec{G}}^{B}=\begin{bmatrix}
L \\
M \\
N
\end{bmatrix}}
\end{array}
$$

# Moment Equations

- Combining the body-axis moments with the angular momentum rate results in the desired moment equations of motion.

$$
\begin{array}{lll}
\begin{array}{c}
\mathbf{\vec{G}}^{b}=\dot{\mathbf{\vec{h}}}^{B}+\Omega \mathbf{\vec{h}}^{B} \\
\mathbf{\vec{h}}^{B}=I^{B} \mathbf{\vec{\omega}}
\end{array} & {\Huge \Rightarrow} & \mathbf{\vec{G}}^{B}=\cancelto{ \rlap{\text{moments of inertia slow to change}} }{ \dot{I}^{B} }\vec{\omega}+I^{B} \dot{\vec{\omega}}+\Omega I^{B}\vec{\omega} \\
\end{array}
\\
\begin{array}{c}
\begin{bmatrix}
L \\
M \\
N 
\end{bmatrix} =\begin{bmatrix}
I_{xx} & -I_{xy}  & -I_{zx} \\
-I_{xy}  & I_{yy}  & -I_{yz} \\
-I_{zx}  & -I_{yz} & I_{zz}
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
\dot{p} \\
\dot{q} \\
\dot{r} 
\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}
0 & -r & q \\
r & 0 & -p \\
-q & p & 0
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
I_{xx} & -I_{xy} & -I_{zx} \\
-I_{xy} & I_{yy} & -I_{yz} \\
I_{zx} & -I_{yz} & I_{zz}
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
p \\
q \\
r
\end{bmatrix} \\
\boxed{\begin{array}{l}
L = I_{xx}\dot{p}-I_{yz}(q^{2}-r^{2})-I_{zx}(\dot{r}+pq)-I_{xy}(\dot{q}-rp)-(I_{yy}-I_{zz})qr \\
M = I_{yy}\dot{q}-I_{zx}(r^{2}-p^{2})-I_{xy}(\dot{p}+qr)-I_{yz}(\dot{r}-pq)-(I_{zz}-I_{xx})rp \\
N = I_{zz}\dot{r}-I_{xy}(p^{2}-q^{2})-I_{yz}(\dot{q}+rp)-I_{zx}(\dot{p}-qr)-(I_{xx}-I_{yy})pq
\end{array}}
\end{array}
$$

# Equations of Motion Summary

- The equations of motion are slightly simplified when the ${}x_{B}{}$ and ${}z_{B}{}$ axes form a plane of symmetry.

$$
\begin{array}{c}
\boxed{
\begin{array}{l}
m(\dot{u}+qw-rv) = F_{x}-mg\sin \theta \\
m(\dot{v}+ru-pw) = F_{y} + mg\cos \theta \sin \phi \\
m(\dot{w}+pv-qu) = F_{z} + mg\cos \theta \cos \phi
\end{array}
}  \\
 \\
\boxed{
\begin{array}{l}
I_{xx}\dot{p}-I_{zx}(\dot{r}+pq)-(I_{yy}-I_{zz})qr=L \\
I_{yy}\dot{q}-I_{zx}(r^{2}-p^{2})-(I_{zz}-I_{xx})rp=M \\
I_{zz}\dot{r}-I_{zx}(\dot{p}-qr)-(I_{xx}-I_{yy})=N
\end{array}
}
\end{array}
$$



# Kinematic Equations

- Vehicle position and attitude are found by the kinematic equations.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
\boxed{\begin{array}{l}
\dot{\phi}=p+q\sin \phi \tan \theta+r\cos \phi \tan \theta \\
\dot{\theta}=q\cos \phi-r\sin \phi  \\
\dot{\psi}=(q\sin \phi+r\cos \phi) /\cos \theta
\end{array}} & \boxed{\begin{array}{l}
\dot{\varphi}=\frac{V_{N}}{R_{E}+h} \\
\dot{\lambda}=\frac{V_{E}}{(R_{E}+h)\cos \varphi} \\
\dot{h}=-V_{D}
\end{array}} 
\end{array}\\
\boxed{\begin{array}{c}
V_{N} = u\cos \theta \cos \psi+v(\sin \phi \sin \theta \cos \psi-\cos \phi \sin \psi)+w(\cos \phi \sin \theta \cos \psi+\sin \phi \sin \psi) \\
V_{E}=u\cos \theta \sin \psi+v(\sin \phi \sin \theta \sin \psi+\cos \phi \cos \psi)+w(\cos \phi \sin \theta \sin \psi-\sin \phi \cos \psi) \\
V_{D}=-u\sin \theta+v\sin \phi \cos \theta+w\cos \phi \cos \theta
\end{array}}
$$


# History Note

- George Hartley Bryan published the following equations of motion in a book titled, "Stability in Aviation" (MacMillan, London, 1911).

$$
\begin{array}{l}
W\left( \frac{du}{gdt}+\frac{qw}{g}-\frac{rv}{g} \right)=W\sin \theta+H-X\quad.\quad. \\
W\left( \frac{dv}{gdt}+\frac{du}{g}-\frac{pw}{g} \right) = W\cos \theta \cos \phi-Y\quad.\quad. \\
W\left( \frac{dw}{gdt}+\frac{pv}{g}-\frac{qu}{g} \right)=-W\cos \theta \sin \phi-Z\quad.\quad. \\
A\frac{dp}{gdt}-F \frac{dq}{gdt}+(C-B) \frac{rq}{g}+F \frac{pr}{g} = -L\quad.\quad. \\
B \frac{dq}{gdt}-F \frac{dp}{gdt}+ (A-C) \frac{pr}{g}-G \frac{qr}{g}= -M\quad.\quad. \\
C \frac{dr}{gdt}+(B-A) \frac{pq}{g}-F \left( \frac{p^{2}-q^{2}}{g} \right)=-Hh -N
\end{array}
\\ \\ \\
\begin{array}{l}
p = \dot{\phi}+\dot{\psi}\sin \theta \\
q=\dot{\theta}\sin \phi +\dot{\psi}\cos \theta \cos \phi \\
r=\dot{\theta}\cos \phi-\dot{\psi}\cos \theta \sin \phi \\
\end{array}
$$
