Slave through the 18th century, bought his own freedom.

Wrote autobiography, where he expresses the stupidity of slavery and his own experience as a slave.

Citation from his book " **Olaudah Equiano,** _**The Interesting Narrative of the Life of Olaudah Equiano**_ (1789)"

"“I believe there are a few events in my life which have not happened to many; it is true the incidents of it are numerous; and, did I consider myself an European, I might say my sufferings were great; but, when I compare my lot with that of most of my countrymen, I regard myself as a particular favourite of Heaven, and acknowledge the mercies of Providence in every occurrence of my life.”
