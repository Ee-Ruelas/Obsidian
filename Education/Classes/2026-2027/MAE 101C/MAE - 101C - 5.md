## Problem

The horizontal roof of a building is coated with tar, which has an emissivity of 0.94. On a cloudy, still night, the ambient air temperature is ${}5\mathrm{\degree C}{}$, and the convective heat transfer coefficient between the air and the roof is estimated to be ${}4 \mathrm{W}/\mathrm{m}^{2}\mathrm{K}{}$. The effective temperature of the sky, acting as a black radiation sink, is ${}-10\mathrm{\degree C}{}$. determine the roof temperature, assuming that the underside of the roof is well insulated.


```math-highlight
varepsilon = 0.94
T_a = (5 + 273.15)K
h_c = 4 W/m^2 K
T_sky = (-10 + 273.15)K
sigma = 5.67E-8 W/m^2 K^4
RHS = h_c*T_a + varepsilon*sigma*T_sky^4
T_s = 270.6K
(varepsilon*sigma*T_s^3 + h_c)*T_s 
```

From iteration you get ${}T_{s}{}$ but that won't be needed in the exams.

## Transient Thermal Response

![[MAE - 101C - 5-1792541407428.webp]]


![[MAE - 101C - 5-1792542769181.webp]]

Reservoir is very large ${}T_{e}={}$const.
So far, we had ${}\frac{dT}{dt}=0{}$ (Steady state)
But if ${}\frac{dT}{dt}\neq0 \Rightarrow{}$ (Transient)
This means we have ${}T(t){}$, so we use: 
"Lumped Thermal Capacity Model"

- The system temp, ${}T{}$ is uniform but changes in time
- Heat transfer process is now time dependent

From energy equation eq.(1.2) in the textbook

(For solid) system ${}\Rightarrow \boxed{\rho \forall c \frac{dt}{dt} = \dot{Q}_{\substack{\text{net}\\ \text{in}}}+\dot{Q}_{\forall}}{}$ but ${}\dot{Q}_{\forall}=0{}$
![[MAE - 101C - 5-1792542736760.webp|246]]
Also, for the liquid:
${}\dot{Q} = \overline{h_{c}}A(T_{(t)}-T_{e}){}$
${}\rho \forall c \frac{dT}{dt}= - \overline{h_{c}}A(T_{(t)}-T_{e}){}$

"Note: the - sign on the R.H.S, since ${}\dot{Q}{}$ leaving the system is negative and it's positive when it goes in"

Goal ${}\Rightarrow{}$ Find ${}T_{(t)}{}$?



$$
\begin{align}
 & \frac{dT}{dt} = \frac{-\overline{h_{c}}A}{\rho \forall c}(T-T_{e})\Rightarrow \frac{dT}{T-T_{e}}=\frac{-\overline{h_{c}}A}{\rho \forall c}dt\\
 & \text{using}\int \frac{dT}{T-T_{e}}=\int \frac{- \overline{h_{c}}A}{\rho \forall c}dt\\ 
 & \boxed{\ln(T-T_{e})=\frac{-\overline{h_{c}}A}{\rho \forall c}t + \overset{\ln(T_{o}-T_{e})}{\text{const.}}}\overset{\substack{\text{Initial}\\ \text{condition}\\ \text{with I.C}}}{\begin{cases}
 t = 0 \\
 T = T_{o}
 \end{cases}} \\
  & \ln(T_{o} - T_{e}) = \frac{- \overline{h_{c}}A}{\rho \forall c}(0) + \text{const.} \\
  & \Rightarrow \text{const.} = \ln(T_{o}-T_{e}) \\
  & \boxed{\ln\left( \frac{T-T_{e}}{T_{o}-T_{e}}\right) = \frac{-\overline{h_{c}}A}{\rho \forall c}t} \\
  & ↪ \frac{T-T_{e}}{T_{o}-T_{e}}= e^{\frac{-\overline{h_{c}}A}{\rho \forall c}t} \equiv e^{-\frac{t}{t_{c}}} \\
  &  \\
  & t_{c} = \text{time const.} = \frac{\rho \forall c}{\overline{h_{c}}A} \\
\end{align}


$$
