# Euler Angles

- Vehicle angular orientation, or attitude, relative to the local NED frame is traditionally specified by three rotations and therefore three Euler angles. 

- The three rotations, transforming from NED frame to body-axis frame, are:
	- Rotation about positive Z-axis by heading angle ${}\twoheadrightarrow \psi{}$
	- Rotation about positive Y-axis by pitch attitude angle ${}\twoheadrightarrow \theta{}$
	- Rotation about positive X-axis by roll attitude angle ${}\twoheadrightarrow \phi{}$
- The rotation order is Z-axis (3-axis), then Y-axis (2-axis), and finally X-axis (1-axis)
	- The Euler angle transformation is sometimes called a "3-2-1" transformation.

# L2B Transformation

- The L2B transformation matrix is therefore defined as follow:
$$
T_{L2B} = T_{1}(\phi)T_{2}(\theta)T_{3}(\psi)=\begin{bmatrix}
1 &  0  & 0  \\
0  &  \cos \phi & \sin \phi  \\
0  & - \sin \phi  & \cos \phi
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
\cos \theta  &  0 &  -\sin \theta \\
0 & 1 &  0 \\
\sin \theta  &  0  & \cos \theta
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
\cos \psi  & \sin \psi & 0 \\
-\sin \psi & \cos \psi & 0  \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$
$$
\boxed{T_{L 2B} = \begin{bmatrix}
\cos \theta \cos \psi   & \cos \theta \sin \psi & -\sin \theta \\
\sin \phi \sin \theta \cos \psi - \cos \theta \sin \psi  &  \sin \phi \sin \theta \sin \psi + \cos \phi \cos \psi  &  \sin \phi \cos \theta \\
\cos \phi \sin \theta \cos \psi+\sin \phi \sin \psi & \cos \phi \sin \theta \sin \psi-\sin \phi \cos \psi  &  \cos \phi \cos \theta
\end{bmatrix}} 

$$
and ${}T_{B2L}= T^{T}_{L2B}{}$ ${}\begin{array}{c}\psi = \text{heading or azimuth angle} \\  \theta= \text{elevation or pitch angle}  \\   \phi = \text{roll or bank angle}\end{array}{}$

# Quaternion Definition

- Euler's Principal Rotation Theorem states that a completely general re-orientation of a rigid body can be accomplished by a single rotation about some fixed axis.
$$
\begin{array}{ll}
\underset{ \underset{ \text{Four Euler Parameters, also known as Quaternions} }{ \uparrow }\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad  }{ \boxed{\begin{bmatrix}
b_{0} \\
b_{x} \\
b_{y} \\
b_{z}
\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}
\cos(\eta/2) \\
B_{x}\sin(\eta /2) \\
B_{y}\sin(\eta / 2) \\
B_{z}\sin(\eta /2)
\end{bmatrix}} }
\begin{array}{c}
\eta = \text{Euler principal angle} \\
\mathbf{\vec{b}}=\begin{bmatrix}
B_{x} \\
B_{y} \\
B_{z}
\end{bmatrix}=\text{unit length principal vector}
\end{array}
\end{array}
$$
- Euler principal axis and angle can be computed once four quaternions are known.
$$
\begin{array}{ll}
\eta=2\cos ^{-1}(b_{0})  & \mathbf{\vec{b}}=\frac{1}{\sin(\eta /2)}\begin{bmatrix}
b_{x} \\
b_{y} \\
b_{z}
\end{bmatrix}
\end{array}
$$
# Normalization

- A constraint equation exists because there are four Euler parameters to define a three-dimensional orientation.
$$
b_{0}^{2}+b_{x}^{2}+b_{y}^{2}+b_{z}^{2}=\cos ^{2}(\eta / 2)+(B^{2}_{x}+B_{y}^{2}+B_{z}^{2})\sin ^{2}(\eta / 2)

$$
But ${}B_{x}^{2}+B_{y}^{2}+B_{z}^{2}=1{}$ and ${}\cos ^{2}(\eta / 2)+\sin ^{2}(\eta /2 )=1{}$
$$
\boxed{b_{0}^{2} + b_{x}^{2}+b_{y}^{2}+b_{z}^{2}=1}

$$

# L2B Transformation Matrix

- The Local-to-Body (L2B) transformation matrix can be written in terms of the four quaternions.
	- No trigonometric functions are required to build the L2B transformation matrix!

$$
\boxed{
T_{L 2B} = \begin{bmatrix}
b_{x}^{2}+b_{0}^{2}-b_{y}^{2}-b_{z}^{2}  & 2(b_{x}b_{y}+b_{z}b_{0})  & 2(b_{x}b_{y}-b_{y}b_{0}) \\
2(b_{x}b_{y}-b_{z}b_{0})  & b_{y}^{2}+b_{0}^{2}-b_{x}^{2}-b_{z}^{2}  &  2(b_{y}b_{z}+b_{x}b_{0}) \\
2(b_{x}b_{z}+b_{y}b_{0})  &  2(b_{y}b_{z}-b_{x}b_{0})  &  b_{z}^{2}+b_{0}^{2} - b_{x}^{2}-b_{y}^{2}
\end{bmatrix}}
$$
and ${}T_{B 2L} = T_{L 2B}^{T}{}$

# Quaternions To Euler Angles

- Conversions from quaternions to Euler angles are obtained by comparing elements of the L2B transformation matrices.
$$
\begin{array}{l} 
T_{L 2 B}=\begin{bmatrix}
b_{x}^{2}+b_{0}^{2}-b_{y}^{2}-b_{z}^{2}  &  2(b_{x}b_{y}+b_{z}b_{0})  & 2(b_{x}b_{z}-b_{y}b_{0}) \\
2(b_{x}b_{y}-b_{z}b_{0})  & b_y^{2}+b_{0}^{2}-b_{x}^{2}-b_{z}^{2} & 2(b_{y}b_{z}+b_{x}b_{0}) \\
2(b_{x}b_{z}+b_{y}b_{0})  & 2(b_{y}b_{z}-b_{x}b_{0}) & b_{z}^{2}+b_{0}^{2}-b_{x}^{2}-b_{y}^{2}
\end{bmatrix} \\
T_{L 2 B}= \begin{bmatrix}
\cos \theta \cos \psi & \cos \theta \sin \psi & -\sin \theta \\
\sin \phi \sin \theta \cos \psi - \cos \phi \sin \psi  &  \sin \phi \sin \theta \sin \psi+\cos \phi \cos \psi & \sin \phi \cos \theta \\
\cos \phi \sin \theta \cos \psi+\sin \phi \sin \psi & \cos \phi \sin \theta \sin \psi-\sin \phi \cos \psi & \cos \phi \cos \theta
\end{bmatrix}  \\
\end{array}
$$
$$
\begin{array}{ccc} 
\boxed{\phi=\tan ^{-1}\left[ \frac{2(b_{y}b_{z}+b_{x}b_{0}}{b_{z}^{2}+b_{0}^{2}-b_{x}^{2}-b_{y}^{2}} \right]} & \boxed{\theta=\sin ^{-1}[2(b_{y}b_{0}-b_{x}b_{z})]} & \boxed{\psi=\tan ^{-1}\left[ \frac{2(b_{x}b_{y}+b_{z}b_{0})}{b_{x}^{2}+b_{0}^{2}-b_{y}^{2}-b_{z}^{2}} \right]}
\end{array}
	$$
