# Velocity

- Differentiate position vectors to find the velocity of point p.
![[MAE - 142 - 5-1793787289405.webp|359]]
$$
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathbf{\vec{r}_{p}}^{E} = \mathbf{\vec{r}_{o}}^{E} + T_{L 2E}\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{L}\\
\dot{\mathbf{\vec{r}}}_{p}^{E} =\dot{\mathbf{\vec{r}}}_{o}^{E}+\dot{T}_{L2E} \mathbf{\vec{r}}^{L}+T_{L 2E} \dot{\mathbf{\vec{r}}}^{L}
\end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
\dot{\mathbf{\vec{r}}}_{o}^{E}=0  & \begin{array}{l} \text{NED origin is not moving \\ relative to ECEF origin} \end{array} \\
\dot{T}_{L 2E}\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{L}\approx 0 & \begin{array}{l} \text{latitude and longitude \\ are changing very slowly} \end{array} 
\end{array}
$$




$$
\begin{array}{lll} 
\boxed{\dot{\mathbf{\vec{r}}}_{p}^{E}=T_{L 2E}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{r}}}^{L}}  & \text{or} & \boxed{\mathbf{\vec{v}}_{p}^{E}=T_{L 2E}\mathbf{\vec{v}}^{L}} & 
\end{array}
$$

# Velocity in ECEF Frame

- Differentiate ECEF position coordinates to find velocity in ECEF frame.

$$
\begin{array}{l}
X= (R_{E}+h)\cos \varphi \cos \lambda \\
Y =(R_{E}+h)\cos \varphi \sin \lambda  \\
Z = (R_{E}+h)\sin \varphi \\
\end{array}
\quad
\begin{array}{c}
\varphi = \text{latitude} \\
\lambda = \text{longitude} \\
h = \text{altitude (above Earth surface)} \\
R_{E} = \text{Earth radius}\approx 6367\,\mathrm{km}  
\end{array}
$$
$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{l}
\dot{X} = \dot{h}\cos \varphi \cos \lambda - (R_{E} + h)\dot{\varphi}\sin \varphi \cos \lambda - (R_{E}+h)\dot{\lambda}\cos \varphi \sin \lambda \\
\dot{Y} = \dot{h}\cos \varphi \sin \lambda - (R_{E}+h)\dot{\varphi}\sin \varphi \sin \lambda + (R_{E}+h)\dot{\lambda}\cos \varphi \cos \lambda \\
\dot{Z} = \dot{h}\sin \varphi+(R_{E}+h)\dot{\varphi}\cos \varphi
\end{array}}
$$
