## Vector Addition in One Frame

- Vectors can be summed directly as long as they are expressed in the same reference frame
![[MAE - 142 - 2-1792346714067.webp|322]]${}\begin{matrix}\mathbf{\vec{r}}_{1}^{A} = \begin{bmatrix}x_{1}^{A} \\  y_{1}^{A} \\  z_{1}^{A}\end{bmatrix} & \mathbf{\vec{r}}_{2}^{A} = \begin{bmatrix}x_{2}^{A} \\  y_{2}^{A} \\  z_{2}^{A}\end{bmatrix}  &   \mathbf{\vec{r}}_{3}^{A} = \begin{bmatrix}x_{3}^{A} \\  y_{3}^{A} \\  z_{3}^{A}\end{bmatrix}\end{matrix}{}$


$$
\boxed{\mathbf{\vec{r}}_{3}^{A}=\mathbf{\vec{r}}_{1}^{A}+\mathbf{\vec{r}}_{2}^{A}}

$$
$$
\mathbf{\vec{r}}_{3}^{A}= \begin{bmatrix}
x_{3}^{A} \\
y_{3}^{A}  \\
z_{3}^{A}\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}
x_{1}^{A}+x_{2}^{A} \\
y_{1}^{A}+y_{2}^{A} \\
z_{1}^{A}+z_{2}^{A}
\end{bmatrix}
$$




## Vector Addition in Two Frames

- Vectors cannot be summed directly if they are expressed in two different reference

![[MAE - 142 - 2-1792347070244.webp|358]]${}\begin{matrix}\mathbf{\vec{r}}_{1}^{A}=\begin{bmatrix}x_{1}^{A} \\  y_{1}^{A} \\  z_{1}^{A}\end{bmatrix} & \mathbf{\vec{r}}_{2}^{B}=\begin{bmatrix}x_{2}^{B} \\  y_{2}^{B} \\  z_{2}^{B}\end{bmatrix} & \mathbf{\vec{r}}_{3}^{A}=\begin{bmatrix}x_{3}^{A} \\  y_{3}^{A} \\  z_{3}^{A}\end{bmatrix}\end{matrix}{}$
$$
\boxed{\mathbf{\vec{r}}_{3}^{A}\neq \mathbf{\vec{r}}_{1}^{A}+\mathbf{\vec{r}}_{2}^{B}}

$$
In other words: They have to be from the same point









## Rotation Matrices

- The orientation of one reference frame relative to another is expressed by a rotation matrix.
- For a positive (right-hand-rule, RHR) rotation about the x-axis or 1-axis:

${}\eta{}$ = rotation angle![[MAE - 142 - 2-1792347404739.webp|641]]

$$\begin{align}x^{B} & =x^{A} \\  y^{B} & =y^{A}\cos \eta+z^{A}\sin \eta \\  z^{B} & =-y^{A}\sin \eta + z^{A}\cos \eta\end{align} \Rightarrow 
\begin{bmatrix}
x^{B} \\
y^{B} \\
z^{B}
\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & \cos \eta & \sin \eta \\
0  & -\sin \eta & \cos \eta
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
x^{A} \\
y^{A} \\
z^{A}
\end{bmatrix}\\
\Rightarrow \boxed{T_{1}(\eta)= \begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & \cos \eta  & \sin \eta \\
0  & -\sin \eta  & \cos \eta
\end{bmatrix}}
$$

## Orthogonal Matrix Properties

- A square matrix is orthogonal if:

$$
\mathbf{AA}^{T}=\mathbf{A}^{T}\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{I} \Rightarrow \mathbf{A}^{T}=\mathbf{A}^{-1}

$$

- Rotation matrices are orthogonal; that is, they only rotate vectors and do not change their length or dimensional size.
$$
T_{1}(\eta)T_{1}^{T}(\eta)=I

$$
$$
T_{1}(\eta) = \begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & \cos \eta & \sin \eta \\
0 & -\sin \eta & \cos \eta
\end{bmatrix}
\Rightarrow
T_{1}^{T}(\eta)=T_{1}^{-1}(\eta)=\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & \cos \eta & -\sin \eta \\
0  & \sin \eta  & \cos \eta
\end{bmatrix}
= T_{1}(-\eta)

$$

## Rotation Between Two Frames

- Rotation between two frames, A and B:
![[MAE - 142 - 2-1792371128394.webp|311]]

  ${}\boxed{\mathbf{\vec{r}}=T_{A2B}\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{A}}{}$ 

${}T^{T}_{A 2B}\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{B}=T^{T}_{A 2B}T_{A 2B}\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{A}=\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{A}{}$

   ${}\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{A}=T^{T}_{A 2B}\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{B}{}$

${}\begin{matrix}\boxed{\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{A}=T_{B 2A}\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{B}} & \boxed{T^{T}_{A 2B}=T_{B 2A}}\end{matrix}{}$








## Rotation Sequences

- Rotation sequences are obtained by multiplying rotation matrices in the appropriate order.

$$
\begin{align}
 & \mathbf{\vec{r}}^{C}=T_{B 2C}\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{B}  \\  
 & \mathbf{\vec{r}}^{C} = T_{B 2C}T_{A 2B}\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{A}
\end{align}
\begin{matrix}
\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{B}=T_{A 2B}\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{A}  \\ \\
\end{matrix}
\quad
\boxed{\begin{matrix}
\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{C}=T_{A 2C}\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{A} \\
T_{A 2C}=T_{B 2C}T_{A 2B}
\end{matrix}}

$$
- The sequence is reversed to convert back from frame C to frame A.

$$
\begin{align}
 & \mathbf{\vec{r}}^{A}=T_{C 2A}\mathbf{\vec{r}}^{C} \\
 & T_{C 2A}=T^{T}_{A 2C}=[T_{B 2C}T_{A 2B}]^{T} = T^{T}_{A 2B}T^{T}_{B 2C} = T_{B 2A}T_{C 2B}
\end{align}

$$

## Vector Addition in Two Frames

- Convert all vectors to the same reference frame and then add.

![[MAE - 142 - 2-1792372226939.webp|295]]
$$
\begin{matrix}

${}\boxed{\mathbf{\vec{r}}_{2}^{B}=T_{A 2B}(\mathbf{\vec{r}}_{3}^{A}-\mathbf{\vec{r}}_{1}^{A})}{}$
