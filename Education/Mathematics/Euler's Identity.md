Euler's identity is an equation that sets the relationship between Euler's number, imaginary numbers and $\pi$. 

**History**
Euler's identity was discovered by Leonhard Euler in the ==mid-18th century==, with its foundational formula published in his 1748 work, '_Introductio in analysin infinitorum_'. While derived around 1740–1748, it is unclear who first wrote the specific, compact form.

$$
e^{i\pi} +1 =0
$$
