**Discussion Questions** to consider for your quotation selection:

1. Where do you see examples of the sublime in Thoreau and Wordsworth? What is the purpose of exploring the sublime for these poets and writers? 
2. How do Victor’s ambitions mirror Walton’s, and what does this suggest about the dangers of ambition unchecked by ethical responsibility?
3. How is the sublime used to explore both human limitations and aspirations? What does the novel suggest about our relationship to nature and our acceptance of our role as natural beings? In what ways is the novel representative of Romantic literature? Compare to Thoreau and Wordsworth.
