# Level Turning Flight

- The forces acting on the airplane are best expressed in a reference frame attached to the vehicle and, therefore, rotating with it.
![[MAE - 142 - 9-1794311201685.webp|244]]Down and ${}z^{R}{}$ are coincident (into page)
$$
\begin{array}{c} 
L = \text{lift} \\
W = \text{weight} \\
\mu = \text{bank angle} \\
V = \text{airspeed} \\
R = \text{turn radius} \\
m = \text{mass}
\end{array}
$$




# Newton's Law in Rotating Frame

- The forces acting on the airplane are represented in the rotating (R) reference frame.

$$
\begin{array}{lllll}
\begin{array}{l}
\text{Forces in\\Rotating \\Frame}
\end{array}  & \mathbf{\vec{F}}^{R}=\begin{bmatrix}
-L\sin \mu \\
T-D & W-L\cos \mu 
\end{bmatrix}
 & \begin{array}{l}
 \text{Velocity in\\Rotating\\Frame}
 \end{array} & \mathbf{\vec{v}}^{R}=\begin{bmatrix}
 0 \\
 V \\
 0
 \end{bmatrix} & 
 \begin{array}{c}
 L = \text{lift} \\
 W = \text{weight} \\
 \mu = \text{bank angle} \\
 V = \text{airspeed} \\
 R = \text{turn radius} \\
 m = \text{mass}
 \end{array}
\end{array}
$$

- Assume the local NED frame is an inertial frame. Newton's Law becomes:

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
m \dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{L} =\mathbf{\vec{F}}^{L}  &  mT_{L 2 R}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{L}=T_{L 2 R}\mathbf{\vec{F}}^{L}= \mathbf{\vec{F}}^{R}  
\end{array}
\\
\boxed{mT_{L 2 R}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{L}= \mathbf{\vec{F}}^{R}}
$$
# Acceleration

- Find the acceleration of the vehicle in the local frame and then rotate the result into the moving frame.

$$
\begin{array}{llll}
 & \mathbf{\vec{v}}^{L}=T_{R 2 L}\mathbf{\vec{v}}^{R} \\
 \text{differentiate} & \dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{L}=T_{R 2 L}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{R}+\dot{T}_{R 2 L}\mathbf{\vec{v}}^{R} \\
 \text{rotate}  & T_{L 2 R}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{L}=T_{L 2 R}T_{R 2 L}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{R}+T_{L 2 R}\dot{T}_{R 2 L}\mathbf{\vec{v}}^{R} \\
  & \boxed{T_{L 2 R}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{L}=\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{R}+T_{L 2 R}\dot{T}_{R 2 L}\mathbf{\vec{v}}^{R}}  & \mathbf{\vec{v}}^{R}=\begin{bmatrix}
  0 \\
  V \\
  0
  \end{bmatrix} & \dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{R}=\begin{bmatrix}
  0 \\
  \dot{V} \\
  0
  \end{bmatrix}
\end{array}
$$

# Moving Frame

- Frame R is obtained after a 3-axis rotation from Frame L.

$$
\begin{array}{lll}
T_{L  2 R}=T_{3}(\chi)=\begin{bmatrix}
\cos \chi  & \sin \chi & 0 \\
-\sin \chi & \cos \chi  & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}  & \to & 
T_{R 2 L}=\begin{bmatrix}
\cos \chi & -\sin \chi & 0 \\
\sin \chi & \cos \chi & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{bmatrix} \\  &  &\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad{\Big\downarrow} \\
 &  & \dot{T}_{R 2 L}=\begin{bmatrix}
 -\dot{\chi}\sin \chi & -\dot{\chi}\cos \chi & 0 \\
 \dot{\chi}\cos \chi & -\dot{\chi}\sin \chi & 0 \\
 0 & 0 & 0
 \end{bmatrix} \\ 
\end{array}\\
\begin{array}{ll}
T_{L 2 R}\dot{T}_{R 2 L}=\begin{bmatrix}
 \cos \chi & \sin \chi & 0 \\
 -\sin \chi & \cos \chi & 0 \\
 0 & 0 & 1
 \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
 -\dot{\chi}\sin \chi & -\dot{\chi}\cos \chi & 0 \\
 \dot{\chi}\cos \chi & -\dot{\chi}\sin \chi & 0 \\
 0 & 0 & 0
 \end{bmatrix}\quad \boxed{T_{L 2 R}\dot{T}_{R 2 L}=\begin{bmatrix}
 0 & -\dot{\chi} & 0 \\
 \dot{\chi} & 0 & 0 \\
 0 & 0 & 0
 \end{bmatrix}}
\end{array}
$$

# Equations of Motion

- Substitute back into Newton's Law to get the final equations of motion.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
mT_{L 2 R} \dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{L}=\mathbf{\vec{F}}^{R}  &   &  & \mathbf{\vec{\dot{v}}}^{R}+T_{L 2 R}\dot{T}_{R 2 L}\mathbf{\vec{v}}^{R}  \\
m[\mathbf{\vec{\dot{v}}}^{R}+T_{L 2 R}\dot{T}_{R 2 L} \mathbf{\vec{v}}^{R}]=\mathbf{\vec{F}}^{R}   &   &  &  \mathbf{\vec{F}}^{R}=\begin{bmatrix}
-L\sin \mu \\
T-D \\
W-L\cos \mu 
\end{bmatrix} \\ \\
 \\
 \\
m\begin{bmatrix}
0 \\
\dot{V} \\
0
\end{bmatrix}+m\begin{bmatrix}
0 & -\dot{\chi} & 0 \\
\dot{\chi} & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
0 \\
V \\
0
\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}
-L\sin \mu \\
T-D \\
W-L\cos \mu
\end{bmatrix} &  &  &  \overset{ \text{3-DOF Level Turn} }{ \boxed{\begin{array}{l}
mV\dot{\chi}=L\sin \mu \\
m\dot{V}=T-D \\
0=W-L\cos \mu 
\end{array}} }
\end{array}
$$

# Body-Axis Velocity

- Vehicle velocity over the ground can also be expressed using the body-axis components.
$$
\begin{array}{lll} 
\mathbf{\vec{v}}^{L}=T_{B 2 L}\mathbf{\vec{v}}^{B}+\mathbf{\vec{w}}^{L} \\
\boxed{\begin{bmatrix}
V_{N} \\
V_{E} \\
V_{D}
\end{bmatrix}=T_{B 2 L}\begin{bmatrix}
u \\
v \\
w
\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}
W_{N} \\
W_{E} \\
W_{D}
\end{bmatrix}}  & \begin{array}{ll} 
\begin{array}{c}
V_{N}=\text{velocity north} & W_{N}=\text{wind north}  \\
V_{E} = \text{velocity east} & W_{E} = \text{wind east} \\
V_{D} = \text{velocity down} & W_{D} = \text{wind down} \\
 \\
u=\text{surge} \\
v=\text{sway} & V=\sqrt{ u^{2}+v^{2}+w^{2} }=\text{airspeed} \\
w=\text{heave}
\end{array}
\end{array}
\end{array}
$$
$$
\begin{bmatrix}
V_{N} \\
V_{E} \\
V_{D}
\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}
\cos \theta \cos \psi & \sin \phi \sin \theta \cos \psi-\cos \phi \sin \psi & \cos \phi \sin \theta \cos \psi+\sin \phi \sin \psi \\
\cos \theta \sin \psi & \sin \phi \sin \theta \sin \psi+\cos \phi \cos \psi & \cos \phi \sin \theta \sin \psi-\sin \phi \cos \psi \\
-\sin \theta & \sin \phi \cos \theta & \cos \phi \cos \theta
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
u \\
v \\
w
\end{bmatrix}
+\begin{bmatrix}
W_{N} \\
W_{E} \\
W_{D}
\end{bmatrix}
$$


# Acceleration in Body Frame

- The acceleration at the body frame origin also includes rotational rate components.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
\begin{array}{l}
\mathbf{\vec{v}}^{L}=T_{B 2 L}\mathbf{\vec{v}}^{B}\quad\text{(array)} \\
\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{L}=T_{B 2 L}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{B}+\dot{T}_{B 2 L}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{B} \\
T_{L 2 B}\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{L}=\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{B}+T_{L 2 B}\dot{T}_{B 2 L}\mathbf{\vec{v}}^{B}
\end{array} & T_{L 2 B}\begin{bmatrix}
\dot{V}_{N} \\
\dot{V}_{E} \\
\dot{V}_{D}
\end{bmatrix}\equiv \mathbf{\vec{a}}^{B}=\begin{bmatrix}
a_{x} \\
a_{y} \\
a_{z}
\end{bmatrix} \\
\boxed{\mathbf{\vec{a}}^{B}=\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{B}+\Omega \mathbf{\vec{v}}^{B}} & T_{L 2 B}\dot{T}_{B 2 L}=\Omega\equiv \begin{bmatrix}
0 & -r & q \\
r & 0 & -p \\
-q & p & 0
\end{bmatrix} \\
\boxed{\begin{bmatrix}
a_{x} \\
a_{y} \\
a_{z}
\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}
\dot{u} \\
\dot{v} \\
\dot{w}
\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}
0 & -r & q \\
r & 0 & -p \\
-q & p & 0
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
u \\
v \\
w
\end{bmatrix}} & \begin{array}{c}
(p,q,r) = \text{rotational rate of body relative local frame} \\
(u,v,w) = \text{velocity in body frame} \\
(a_{x},a_{y},a_{z}) = \text{acceleration in body frame}
\end{array}
\end{array}
$$

# Accelerometers

- An accelerometer measures applied force. which is not quite the body-axis acceleration.

$$
\begin{array}{c}
\mathbf{\vec{a}}^{B}=\dot{\mathbf{\vec{v}}}^{B}+\Omega \mathbf{\vec{v}}^{B}-T_{L 2 B}\begin{bmatrix}
0 \\
0 \\
g
\end{bmatrix} \\
 \\
\boxed{\begin{bmatrix}
a_{x} \\
a_{y} \\
a_{z} \\
\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}
\dot{u} \\
\dot{v} \\
\dot{w} 
\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}
0 & -r & q \\
r  & 0 & -p  \\
-q & p & 0
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
u \\
v \\
w
\end{bmatrix}-\begin{bmatrix}
-g\sin \theta \\
g\cos \theta \sin \phi \\
g\cos \theta \cos \phi
\end{bmatrix}}
\end{array}

$$

# Roll and Pitch Euler Angles

- Revisit attitude determination problem and include body frame rotation.

$$
\begin{array}{c}
\begin{bmatrix}
a_{x} \\
a_{y} \\
a_{z}
\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}
\dot{u} \\
\dot{v} \\
\dot{w} 
\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}
0 & -r & q \\
r & 0 & -p \\
-q & p & 0
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
u \\
v \\
w
\end{bmatrix}-\begin{bmatrix}
-g\sin \theta \\
g\cos \theta \sin \phi \\
g\cos \theta \cos \phi
\end{bmatrix} \\
 \\
\begin{array}{lll}
\begin{array}{l}
\dot{u}\approx 0,\dot{v}\approx 0,\dot{w}\approx 0 \\
u\approx V,v \approx 0,w\approx 0
\end{array}  & {\Huge\Rightarrow} & \begin{bmatrix}
a_{x} \\
a_{y} \\
a_{z}
\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}
0 \\
rV \\
-qV
\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}
g\sin \theta \\
-g\cos \theta \sin \phi \\
-g\cos \theta \cos \phi
\end{bmatrix}
\end{array} \\
 \\
\begin{array}{ll}
\boxed{\theta=\tan ^{-1}\left[ \frac{a_{x}}{\sqrt{ (a_{y}-rV)^{2}+(a_{z}+qV)^{2} }} \right]} & \boxed{\phi=\tan ^{-1}\left[ \frac{-(a_{y}-rV)}{-(a_{z}+qV)} \right]}
\end{array}
\end{array}
$$
